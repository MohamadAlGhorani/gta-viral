Privacy Statement

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This statement explains how GTA Viral handles personal data on gtaviral.com and on its social media channels, in line with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, in Dutch: AVG). We keep it simple: this site has no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no cookies.

1. Who is responsible

The controller for the processing of personal data described in this statement is:

  • Mohamad Al Ghorani, private individual, the Netherlands
  • Email: contact@gtaviral.com

GTA Viral is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

2. What data we process and why

Visiting the website (hosting). The site is hosted by Netlify, Inc. When you visit, Netlify's servers automatically process technical data such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the date and time. This is needed to deliver the site and keep it secure. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a working, secure website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Netlify acts as our processor. Under its data processing agreement, Netlify keeps these access logs online for 30 days and in archived storage for up to 13 months.

Emailing us. If you email contact@gtaviral.com, we receive your email address, name (if you give it) and the content of your message. We use this only to answer you, for example about a clip removal request. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering your message (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We delete these emails no later than 12 months after the conversation has ended, unless we need them longer to handle a legal claim or a copyright request.

People who appear in our clips. On our social media channels we share short clips from public livestreams. A clip can contain personal data of the people in it, such as their channel name, their voice and, if they stream with a camera, their image. We do not get this data from you directly: it comes from the public livestream. We use it only to share the moment and to credit the streamer. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in sharing notable moments from public streams (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). A clip stays online until we remove it, or until you ask us to remove it (see "Your rights" below).

Links to social media. This site links to our TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Facebook pages. These are plain links: no content from those platforms is embedded, and no data is shared with them until you click. Once you visit those platforms, their own privacy policies apply.

Our pages on those platforms. When you view, follow or react to our pages, the platform collects data about you under its own privacy policy. We only see totals, such as the number of views and followers. For our Facebook page, Meta and we are jointly responsible for those page statistics; how that responsibility is divided is set out in Meta's Page Insights Controller Addendum.

3. Cookies and tracking

This website does not place any cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. That is why you do not see a cookie banner. Fonts, images and scripts are all served from our own domain, so your browser does not contact any other company when you visit.

4. Sharing and transfers outside the EU

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the companies that provide our hosting and our mailbox. Clips are published on the social media platforms named above. Netlify is based in the United States, so data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area. Netlify is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and its data processing agreement falls back on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses if that framework stops applying.

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling.

5. Security

The site is served only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). We keep the amount of data we process as small as possible.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have incorrect data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • have the processing of your data restricted;
  • receive your data in a portable format.

Your right to object. Everything we do with personal data is based on our legitimate interests, so you can object to it at any time. If you appear in one of our clips and want it gone, send us a link to the clip and we will remove it.

To use any of these rights, email contact@gtaviral.com. We reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

7. Complaints

If you are not happy with how we handle your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

8. Children

This site is about Grand Theft Auto VI, a game rated PEGI 18, and is not aimed at children. If you are under 16, please do not send us personal data without permission from a parent or guardian.

9. Changes

We may update this statement, for example when the site changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
